> Explain important factors of physical installations.

- Technology designed for indoor environmental comfort that provides temperature control, humidity management, and airflow regulation
- Important for hardware
## Temperature Control
- Fundamental consideration as computer networks and data centers house electronic equipment that generates heat
- Most networking equipment has failsafes designed to power off if it gets too hot to prevent any damage from occurring
- To prevent overheating, maintain a stead temperature of 68-77^o F (20-25^o C)
	- Check manufacturer recommendations for specific temperatures
## Humidity
- Concentration of water vapor present in the air
- Water bad for electricity
	- Not enough water also bad?
- Too much humidity will create condensation
	- Will cause corrosion or electrical shorts in equipment
- Too little humidity can cause static electrical discharge
	- Can damage electronics
- Ideal humidity level is 40-60%
## Airflow Management
- Heat generation must be managed and dissipated
### Port-side Exhaust and Intake (Hot/Cold Aisle)
- Strategic method of air distribution in which server tacks are positioned in alternating rows with cold and hot air
