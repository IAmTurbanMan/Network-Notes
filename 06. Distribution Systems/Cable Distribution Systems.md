## Demarcation Point
- Location at which the Internet service provider's connection ends and the network infrastructure and cabling begin
- Signifies the entrance where the WAN connection enter's the facility
	- In most office buildings, there is a telecommunications box
- ISP is responsible for everything working up until this point
	- Everything after is the organization's responsibility
- Will be located within the MDF
## MDF (Main Distribution Frame)
- Serves as the main starting point for all interior cabling that will be distributed throughout the facility
- Will find main presence router and backbone switch here
	- Backbone switch is where everything else in the network connects to
		- Small networks may have one 24-port switch
## IDF (Intermediate Distribution Frame)
- Many of these can be connected back to the MDF
- Larger organizations may need multiple 48-port switches
	- Will connect back to MDF backbpone
