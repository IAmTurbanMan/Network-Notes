> Given a scenario, select and configure wireless devices and technologies.

- Devices designed to send and receive radio frequency signals
## Omnidirectional Antennas
- Designed to transmit and receive wireless signals in all directions equally
- Great for covering a broad area with a relatively uniform signal strength in all directions
- Default type of antenna used by WAPs and wireless cards, etc.
## Unidirectional Antennas
- Focuses on a single direction to provide a more concentrated signal beam
- Best used when majority of the signal needs to be directed at a specific area or when trying to reach a remote area from a centralized location
- Found in wireless [[Wireless Network Types#Point-to-point|point-to-point]] connection
- Suitable for long and narrow coverage areas or a strong signal in one particular direction
## Yagi Antennas
- Specific type of directional antenna that can provide high signal gains and uses a narrow beamwidth
- Excellent for long distance communication in a specific direction
- Often used in remote areas to connect to a far off cell tower or for a television transmitter
- Best when capturing or sending signals over long distances to or from a specific, fixed location
