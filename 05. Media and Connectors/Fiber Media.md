> Compare and contrast transmission media and transceivers.

- Used to transmit data using light instead of electrical impulses
- Fiber cable is usually not the bottlenecks in the networks anymore
## Advantages
- EMI immunity
	- EMI only caused by electrical signals, and can only effect other electrical signals
		- Fiber uses light
	- Doesn't need a shielded cable to overcome EMI interference
- Extended transmission range
- Higher data transfer speeds
	- 10 - 100 Gbps
## Disadvantages
- Cost
	- 25 ft ~= $15 - $20
	- Equivalent length copper cable ~= $5
	- Network equipment that supports fiber is also more expensive
- Complexity
	- Requires special tools and training
## Types
### Single-Mode Fiber
- Designed for long-distance communication and features a small glass core typically around 8.3 to 10 microns in diameter
