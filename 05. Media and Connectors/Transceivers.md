> Compare and contrast transmission media and transceivers.


> [!TIP] Transceiver
> Generalized term for any device that can both transmit and receive data

- A transceiver can convert or translate the data sent by one protocol to another protocol
	- Ethernet <-> Fiber Channel
- Can convert network connections between different types, even when the protocol is the same
- Convert long-distance fiber connections (between two buildings) into a copper media connection
- Considered to be [[Layer 1 (Physical)|Layer 1]] devices
	- Can perform any Layer 1 to Layer 1 conversions
		- Copper <-> Fiber
		- Copper <-> Coaxial
		- Coaxial <-> Fiber
		- Copper <-> Wireless
		- etc.
## Form Factors
### SFP (Small Form Factor Pluggable)
- Compact, hot pluggable optical module that's used with fiber connections
- Supports up to 4.25 Gbps
### SFP+ (Small Form Factor Pluggable Plus)
- Faster version of SFP
	- 16 Gbps
### QSFP (Quad Small Form Factor Pluggable)
- Compact and hot pluggable
- Supports around 4 Gbps
### QSFP+ (Quad Small Form Factor Pluggable Plus)
- 40 Gbps
### QSFP28 (Quad Small Form Factor Pluggable 28)
- 100 Gbps
### QSFP56 (Quad Small Form Factor Pluggable 56)
- 200 Gbps
