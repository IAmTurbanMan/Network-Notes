> Compare and contrast transmission media and transceivers.


> [!TIP] Transceiver
> Generalized term for any device that can both transmit and receive data

- A transceiver can convert or translate the data sent by one protocol to another protocol
	- Ethernet <-> Fiber Channel
- Can convert network connections between different types, even when the protocol is the same
- Convert long-distance fiber connections (between two buildings) into a copper media connection
