## Connectors
### SC (Subscriber Connector)
- Known for its square shape and push-pull design that offers a secure and simple connection mechanism
- "Stick and Click Connector"
- Durable and easy to use
- Used in FTTH (Fiber to the Home) deployments
### LC (Lucent Connector)
- Distinguished by a compact size that utilizes a push-pull mechanism similar to the SC but in a much smaller form factor
- High density applications
	- Data centers and telecommunications rooms
	- Smaller size allows for greater density on panels and switches
- "Love Connector"
	- Two cable pair and connectors are bound together
	- Transmit and receive sides are joined together
### ST (Straight Tip Connector)
- Recognizable by its round shape and twist-lock mechanism that offers a robust and reliable connector type for use in networks
- "Stick and Twist Connector"
- Great for environments where movement and vibrations can occur
- Commonly used with MMF cables and applications
- Used in campus network environments
- Durability makes it suitable for outdoor applications
- Easy installation
### MTRJ (Mechanical Transfer-Registered Jack)
- Features a small, rectangular design and houses both the transmit and receive fibers in a single connector
- Connecting devices in a LAN
- Well suited to connecting workstations and servers
- Dual-fiber connector minimizes space and offers a cost-effective solution for dense networks
### MPO (Multi-Fiber Push On)
- Designed to accommodate multiple fibers in a single connector to significantly increase the capacity and flexibility of the fiber networks
- 12+ fibers in a single MPO
- Used for backbone cabling
- Simultaneous handling of multiple fibers is crucial in modern data centers
## Polishes
### PC (Physical Contact)
- Slight curvature in face of the fiber for a lower back reflection
- Commonly used in digital and telephone services
- Provide the least effective reduction in back reflection
	- Best used over short distance or lower speed data transmission network
		- Back reflection is less of a concern
### UPC (Ultra Physical Contact)
- Dome shaped end face for better core alignment
- Offers lower back reflection than PC style
- General broadband, data, and video applications
### APC (Angled Physical Contact)
- 8^o angled polish to greatly reduce back reflection
- Lowest amount of back reflection
- Highly suited for long distance and high bandwidth applications
	- Used in long-haul fiber installations
		- Undersea cable networks
