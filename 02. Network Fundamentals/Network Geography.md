> Compare and contrast network topologies, architectures, and types.
### PAN (Personal Area Network)
- Smallest type of wired or wireless network
- Covers distance of 10 feet or less
- Bluetooth, USB, NFC
	- USB serial connection is considered a network
### LAN (Local Area Network)
- Connects components in a limited distance
- Up to 100 m/300 ft
- Examples
	- Internal networks at school or work
	- Home network
### CAN (Campus Area Network)
- A building-centric LAN that is spread across numerous buildings in a certain area
- Different buildings linked together on the same network
	- College campus, business park, military bases
- Can cover several miles across many different buildings
### MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
- Connects locations that are scattered across an entire city
- Up to 25 miles, depending on the size of the city
- Examples
	- Colleges with multiple locations across the city
	- Governmental departments like the DMV
### WAN (Wide Area Network)
- Connects geographically disparate internal networks
- Biggest network
- Lease Lines and VPNs
- Examples
	- The internet
	- Companies that are scattered across the globe
	- US Gov't
- Does not always have to be public
